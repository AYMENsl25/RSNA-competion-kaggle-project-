# Development split frozen for blinded report sampling only — 2026-10-09

Astra reviewed candidate v3 and approved a **sampling-only freeze**. The immutable private copy is `outputs/frozen_split_sampling_v1/manifest.csv`, SHA-256 `b5926bd9d82154debee602a1824cf874aed1d0f409386308a387ed6a2f49debc`. It contains 3,457 training, 885 validation and 65 excluded studies. Its reproducible construction and edge checks are in [CANDIDATE_SPLIT_V3.md](CANDIDATE_SPLIT_V3.md) and `candidate_grouped_split_v3_audit.json`.

All 1,101 detected near-report links and 171 unresolved image links remain within partitions; combined expert-connected components are excluded. This is **operational containment of detected candidates**, not proof that patients are independent. The 101 sampled-dissimilar dHash links were omitted under a documented limited-evidence decision. Sparse image screening, skipped hash buckets and study-specific deidentified PatientIDs leave residual linkage risk.

The local blinded packets are `outputs/report_review_packets_v1/packet_A.csv` (60 training-side rule-review reports) and `packet_B.csv` (120 held-out reference reports). Reviewers see no UID, partition, group, selection stratum, candidate assertion or model output. A private mapping holds that information. Each packet contains one study per current connected component. The deterministic quotas, enrichment rule, inputs and packet hashes are recorded in `report_review_packet_manifest.json`. Packet files contain deidentified report text and remain in ignored local outputs; they are not sent to reviewers by this study process.

Validation reviewer judgments must not guide training-side labeler revisions. If any split change becomes necessary, both packets and any annotations tied to them must be invalidated and regenerated under a new version. This freeze does **not** authorize image-model training: only three targets have provisional validation 20/20 assertion support, no independent reviewed reference exists yet, and integrated GPU runtime has not been measured. The 58 protected expert studies remain excluded and unopened for model selection.
