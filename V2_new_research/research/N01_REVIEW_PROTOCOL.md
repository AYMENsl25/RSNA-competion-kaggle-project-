# Report assertion review protocol (sampling-only split frozen; annotations pending)

Purpose: determine whether reports can support the organizer's twelve image-defined findings without treating silence or generic pathology words as negative/positive labels. This reviews **report text**, not MRI truth. It does not use the 58 protected expert studies.

1. Use the [sampling-only frozen split](FROZEN_SPLIT_SAMPLING_V1.md), SHA-256 `b5926bd9d82154debee602a1824cf874aed1d0f409386308a387ed6a2f49debc`. Never sample a reference from a later changed split without invalidating the old packet and annotations.
2. Use candidate-training reports to develop multilingual assertion rules. Start with at most 60 language-stratified reports, including target mentions, severity words, negation, uncertainty and temporal/surgical language. A competent reviewer for each language used must inspect the original text, not an automatic translation alone. Keep all reviewer notes and report text in ignored local outputs.
3. After rules and split are fixed, draw a prespecified language-stratified **candidate-validation** review sample of 120 reports. Enrich rare targets from the lexical inventory only under a recorded sampling rule; report the resulting target and language distribution. If the predeclared support gate remains short, allow one additional fixed batch of at most 120. All reviewers remain blinded to the candidate extractor, image predictions and the 58 expert labels.
4. For each report and finding, record one of `positive`, `negative`, `uncertain`, `not_addressed`, plus the exact supporting span, language, reviewer and rubric reason. Use the [host finding definitions](LABEL_RUBRIC.md); unspecified severity, history, possible findings and generic degeneration remain `uncertain` or `not_addressed` where the definition is not met. A second reviewer adjudicates threshold-sensitive or conflicting cells. Unresolved disagreements stay masked. Reviewers see only an opaque review ID, detected language, original report and blank annotation fields; partition, selection stratum, study/group IDs, candidate assertions and model outputs remain in a private map. Validation judgments must not guide revisions to the training-side labeler.
5. Check extractor precision and recall against this independent reviewed reference by target and language. Freeze reference file, masks, split, sampling rule and SHA-256 hashes before M00/M01. The full twelve-target comparison requires at least 20 adjudicated positive and 20 negative cells for **each target in both development training and independent validation**. An enriched review sample is a selected evaluation population, so its AUC cannot be presented as cohort-representative without adjustment.

The current 110-report language sample includes 81 candidate-training and 29 candidate-validation reports and is useful only for exploratory routing. It is not the independent frozen reference. The current lexical inventory counts cue co-occurrences but supplies no labels. If qualified multilingual or clinical review is unavailable, the label-quality gate remains failed; the study must not proceed to full M00 training under the approved design.

`src/research/make_report_review_packets.py` wrote local, ignored `packet_A.csv` and `packet_B.csv` after Astra approved the sampling-only freeze. It requires the exact manifest SHA-256 and a quarantine list. The first pass samples 60 training-side reports for rule development and 120 held-out reports for a blinded reference, with fixed language quotas, one study per connected component, and a recorded balance of lexical enrichment and hashed random controls. It generates blank state/evidence fields and separate private UID maps; it does not infer labels or send reports to reviewers. Packet and source hashes are in `report_review_packet_manifest.json`.
