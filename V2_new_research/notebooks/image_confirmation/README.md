# Private CPU confirmation audit

Prepared for root review; this embedded-payload method was **not pushed**. Automatic approval review rejected uploading a generated script containing study and series identifiers. The generated code file and kernel metadata were removed. Use `../image_confirmation_safe/confirm.py`, which derives selection from Kaggle-mounted official data and the preceding private Kaggle screen at runtime, with no embedded study identifiers in the uploaded source. The audit does not change any split or train a model.

`prepare.py` reads the current ignored dHash edge file (272 pairs), candidate manifest, and official series metadata. It never reads reports or expert label cells. It embeds the fixed pairs and first-screened series metadata into standalone `confirm.py`, with source and payload hashes. Expert pixel quarantine contains the 58 expert UIDs from manifest exclusion reasons. The one expert-linked pair runs first with headers only, followed by the 100 cross-fold candidate pairs, then remaining candidates and 100 controls.

Controls are sampled without replacement as unique pairs from nonexcluded studies, using `random.Random(20261009)` and sorted UIDs. Candidate pairs are excluded. First-screened series must match official plane, fluid-sensitive, and fat-suppression metadata. Selection is fixed before image metrics; controls will not tune thresholds.

The private internet-off CPU script reads series headers, checks UID/path agreement and within-series geometry, and sorts by orientation-projected physical slice position. It compares five distinct slices at fixed 15%, 32.5%, 50%, 67.5%, and 85% depth. Pair comparability requires matching sequence metadata, row/column direction dots >=0.99, physical field-of-view deltas <=10%, and coverage span ratio <=1.20. Otherwise the pair is unresolved. Selected images are normalized independently by full-slice 1st/99th percentiles, resized preserving physical aspect, and centrally cropped to 320×320. No registration, flipping, learned method, or hyperparameter fitting is performed.

At least three slices simultaneously passing NCC >=0.99, normalized RMSE <=0.035 and SSIM >=0.97 produces **high similarity requiring manual confirmation**. At least three slices with NCC <0.90 and SSIM <0.75 produces **sampled slices dissimilar**. Other comparable results remain unresolved. These predeclared thresholds are conservative audit heuristics and do not establish patient identity. Quantile alignment can miss anatomical correspondence, and dissimilar sampled slices do not rule out duplication elsewhere.

The script is bounded at 1,500 seconds and 3,800 decodes; incomplete pairs and failures remain explicit. Outputs contain aggregate decisions and slice-metric percentiles by candidate/control/cross-fold strata, source/script/payload hashes, decoder counts, elapsed time, comparison policy, and limitations. No UIDs, reports, image exports, pair-level results, or label values are written or printed. Expert pixels are prohibited in the loader. Expert labels and `train.csv` are never loaded. Candidate/control UIDs exist only in the private script's fixed input.

The original `prepare.py` and template remain as a record of the rejected design. Do not generate or upload its embedded payload. The safe version has its own private Kaggle metadata and contains no local study identifiers.
